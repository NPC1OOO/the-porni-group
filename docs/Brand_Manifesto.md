## 1. Cultural & Anti-Corporate Philosophy
While mainstream AI ventures burn billions training timid chatbots to compose corporate resignation letters, optimize motivational LinkedIn platitudes, or build yet another B2B productivity dashboard, **The Porni Group** attacks the computational problems everyone else is too bashful to name:
- **The True Frontier**: The human body is biology's pinnacle of physical engineering. Simulating deformable soft-tissue, accurate anatomical dynamics, and temporal stability is one of the hardest problems in computer science.
- **Audacity with Rigour**: We unapologetically call it **boob physics**, but our research encompasses GPU-accelerated mass-spring lattices, finite element deformable geometry, and neural rendering integration.
- **Zero Sycophancy**: We refuse to surrender controlling equity to venture capitalists whose crowning technological achievement was configuring Microsoft Teams.
- **Honest Ambition**: We are three early-stage founders with an audacious £30,000,000 target. We offer no illusions of imaginary revenue; we offer frontier research and technical domination.

## 3. Ethical Synthetic Creation
The Porni Group establishes three core ethical tenets for enterprise generative media:

### I. Transparent Neural Attribution
We do not mask the synthetic origin of our work. Every still, cinematic sequence, and simulated mesh explicitly cites its underlying checkpoint, sampling parameters, and geometric constraints.

### II. Symbiosis Over Replacement
Machines expand the boundaries of the human mind and physical imagination. We use neural models and physical simulations to execute impossible visions that physical cameras and static rigs cannot grasp.

### III. C2PA Cryptographic Content Authenticity
All enterprise and research media assets are sealed with immutable SHA-256 provenance hashes adhering to the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) framework.
