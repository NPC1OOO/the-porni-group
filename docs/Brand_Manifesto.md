# The Synthetic Manifesto // The Porni Group

> [!NOTE] Official Creed
> *"We reject the simulation of mediocrity. Synthetic intelligence is not an imitation of nature, but the birth of an autonomous aesthetic geometry."*

## 1. Aesthetic DNA & Brand Tone
- **Tone**: Provocative, unapologetic, technologically sophisticated, museum-grade luxury (Awwwards / FWA standard).
- **Surface Hierarchy**:
  - `Base`: OLED Pure Black (`#08080A`)
  - `Card Surface`: Dark Zinc (`#101014`, `#16161C`) with subtle glassmorphism (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.75)`)
  - `Accents`:
    - Cyber Cyan: `#00F0FF` (Primary logic, links, active nodes)
    - Neon Magenta: `#FF007F` (Energy, cinema, tactile tension)
    - Acidic Lime: `#DFFF00` (Status, C2PA cryptographic verification)
- **Typography Matrix**:
  - Primary Sans: `Space Grotesk` (Grotesque precision, wide apertures)
  - Secondary Mono: `JetBrains Mono` (Technical telemetry, parameters, seeds)

## 2. Ethical Synthetic Creation
The Porni Group establishes three core ethical tenets for enterprise generative media:

### I. Transparent Neural Attribution
We do not mask the synthetic origin of our work. Every still, cinematic sequence, and acoustic waveform explicitly cites its underlying checkpoint, sampling steps, and CFG guidance.

### II. Human-Machine Symbiosis
AI does not replace human taste; it amplifies it by expanding the latent search space. The artist operates as curator, director, and latent navigator.

### III. C2PA Cryptographic Content Authenticity
All enterprise commercial assets are sealed with immutable SHA-256 provenance hashes adhering to the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) framework.
