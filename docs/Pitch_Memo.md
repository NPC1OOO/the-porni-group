# Confidential Investor Pitch Memo // The Porni Group

> [!NOTE] Executive Summary
> **Founding Team**: 3 Founders  
> **Target Round**: £30,000,000 Seed Investment  
> **Core Objective**: GPU-Accelerated Soft-Body Simulation, Deformable Geometry, Anatomically Consistent Video Synthesis, and Neural Rendering Integration ("Boob Physics")  
> **Commercial Target**: High-fidelity digital humans across interactive entertainment, digital media, and computer graphics.  
> **Equity Proposition**: Compute-backed equity allocation for resilient investors. Zero tolerance for £40k podcast VCs offering Microsoft Teams wisdom.

## The Pitch Memo (Verbatim)

Dear Investors,

Adults wanna come, and I wanna drain their wallets. Welcome to Porni Group, where our ambitions are enormous, our restraint is questionable, and our enthusiasm for artificial intelligence borders on a diagnosable condition. We're three founders with an appetite for technological innovation, commercial domination, and the sort of scientific research that would make a university ethics committee quietly close its doors. While other AI startups are busy teaching chatbots to compose resignation letters and generate motivational LinkedIn posts, we've identified what we believe to be a criminally underfunded frontier of computational science: boobs and dicks.

Yes, you read that correctly. Boobs and dicks. The human body is an extraordinary feat of biological engineering, yet generative AI still struggles to reproduce its anatomy, proportions, movements and physical interactions with any degree of consistency. Synthetic characters routinely suffer from anatomical distortions, temporal instability, unrealistic soft-tissue behaviour and enough visual inconsistencies to make an experienced animator contemplate early retirement. We believe these limitations represent genuine technical problems in generative modelling, computational geometry, physically based animation and synthetic character systems. Unfortunately for our future academic credibility, we've decided to summarise this entire field of research as **boob physics**.

> [!IMPORTANT] The Research Programme
> Our proposed research programme would investigate:
> - High-fidelity synthetic character generation
> - GPU-accelerated soft-body simulation
> - Anatomically consistent video synthesis
> - Deformable geometry and non-linear elasticity
> - Temporal coherence across extended sequences
> - Realistic human movement and physical collision manifolds
>
> We want to explore how neural rendering can be integrated with physical simulation to improve the consistency of digitally generated humans across extended sequences. If we eventually develop a system capable of accurately simulating complex anatomical structures without requiring an industrial supercomputer, we intend to commercialise the underlying technology across digital media, interactive entertainment and computer graphics. Naturally, we'll be celebrating this scientific achievement by telling everyone we spent millions researching boobs.

Now, let's discuss the small matter of funding. We're seeking **£30,000,000**, because apparently every other AI company can raise an obscene amount of money to make a chatbot marginally more agreeable, but the moment three entrepreneurs propose scientifically investigating anatomical simulation, everyone suddenly becomes financially responsible. Serious computational research requires substantial processing capacity, specialist engineering talent, experimental infrastructure and sustained development. We envisage a research organisation with dedicated GPU infrastructure, rigorous evaluation methods and an unreasonable collection of technical reports whose titles will sound considerably more respectable than our internal Discord conversations.

Of course, we also understand that capital isn't free, which brings us to the subject of ownership. **You want some of my precious equity? Fund me so I can research boobs and dicks!** We're not surrendering controlling interests in our company because someone with a venture capital podcast offered us £40,000, a networking opportunity and the inspirational wisdom of a man whose greatest technical accomplishment was installing Microsoft Teams. We want investors who understand the difference between speculative research and meaningless hype, who recognise the commercial potential of better synthetic animation, and who possess the extraordinary emotional resilience required to explain Porni Group to their investment committee.

> [!WARNING] Honest Pre-Seed Status & Reality Check
> We're not going to insult your intelligence by claiming that our three-founder venture currently possesses a revolutionary proprietary model, millions in annual revenue or a fully staffed research laboratory. We are early-stage, our technical ambitions are substantial, and our £30 million target represents a proposed long-term financing objective rather than an established company valuation. We recognise that any serious investment would require credible milestones, a research budget, commercial validation and technical demonstrations. However, we refuse to pretend that the correct way to build an ambitious technology company is to sound like every other startup begging investors to fund another AI-powered productivity dashboard.

Porni Group intends to build real technology, pursue difficult research and establish a distinctive position within generative media. Our branding may be outrageous, our investor correspondence may horrify your compliance department, and our eventual research publications may require unusually careful wording, but the computational challenges we're interested in solving are legitimate. If you're looking for a timid founding team that will spend six months choosing a shade of blue for its corporate website, you've come to the wrong people. If you're interested in supporting an ambitious programme investigating the physical realism of synthetic humans, then perhaps we have something worth discussing.

We want thirty million pounds, a formidable computing infrastructure, and the opportunity to investigate some of the most technically challenging aspects of digital human simulation. You want equity, we want GPUs, and somewhere between those two interests lies the possibility of building a commercially valuable technology company. We're not promising miracles, but we're certainly not going to apologise for having ambitions that exceed the size of our current balance sheet.

And because every investment pitch apparently needs an irresistible promise, here's ours:

> [!TIP] The Irresistible Promise
> **"If you invest, we'll guarantee you can wank at a clicks notice ;)"**  
> *Consider that a statement of our extraordinarily questionable sense of humour, rather than a contractual guarantee.*

Regards,  
**The Founders**  
Porni Group
